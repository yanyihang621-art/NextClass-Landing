# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Students in universities across mainland China. They seek an efficient, ad-free way to manage and import their course schedules from their university's educational administration systems.

## Product Purpose
To provide a dedicated, pure, and highly efficient course schedule manager. It allows one-click automatic schedule extraction without any advertisements or bloatware. Success means users download the APK and enjoy a seamless scheduling experience.

## Brand Personality
Pure/Ad-free (无广告), Smooth/Fluid (流畅丝滑), Clean/Minimalist (简洁). The tone is utilitarian, highly polished, and distinctly anti-bloatware.

## Anti-references
- AI-generated clichés: Overused glassmorphism (毛玻璃), excessive rounded floating windows (圆角浮窗), and generic AI-style small icons (like the Magic Wand for automation).
- Poor mobile layouts that cause text or elements to overflow or misalign.
- Typical bloated university portal pages with chaotic information density.

## Design Principles
- **Clarity over decoration**: Ditch the artificial glass and excessive glows; use crisp borders and solid contrasts.
- **Fluid but grounded**: Interactions should feel smooth and native, not floaty or disconnected.
- **Content is king**: The interface should get out of the way to highlight core features like one-click extraction.
- **Functionality is sacred**: Functional logic (like the APK download behavior) must remain intact and reliable across all visual overhauls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Ensure high contrast for readability and mobile-first responsive layout that works flawlessly across varying device sizes (no horizontal scroll or clipping).
